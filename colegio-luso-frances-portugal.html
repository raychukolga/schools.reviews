Colégio Luso-Francês is an international school located in Porto founded in 1936. The school follows the Portuguese national curriculum curriculum, with Portuguese as the primary language of instruction.

Recent students have placed in the top 10 at the National Informatics Olympiad and featured in the Portuguese Mathematics Olympiad. The school has its own Francisco de Assis Auditorium, open to the wider community.

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. Additional languages offered include English, French (?).
