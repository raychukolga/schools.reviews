Colégio Luso-Francês is a private Portuguese Catholic school in Porto, founded in 1936 and run by the Franciscan Missionaries of Our Lady. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to the end of secondary school, and is co-educational.

Recent students have placed in the top 10 at the National Informatics Olympiad and featured in the Portuguese Mathematics Olympiad. The school has its own Francisco de Assis Auditorium, open to the wider community.

Children join at age 3 (three years of pre-school) and can stay until Grade 12. In secondary the school offers four tracks — Sciences and Technology, Socio-economic Sciences, Languages and Humanities, and Visual Arts — leading to the national exams and Portuguese universities. English is taught throughout, with German as an extra option in secondary.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Luso-Francês follow?
Colégio Luso-Francês follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12. Secondary pupils choose one of four tracks and sit the national exams.
How much does Colégio Luso-Francês cost per year?
Annual tuition for 2026/27 ranges from €3,950 (pre-school) to €5,900 (secondary), paid in 10 monthly instalments. New pupils pay €60 for pre-registration and €350 for enrollment; returning pupils pay €300 a year to re-enroll.
What ages does Colégio Luso-Francês accept?
Colégio Luso-Francês takes children from age 3 (pre-school) to 18 (Grade 12). It is co-educational. Please contact the school to confirm places for a specific year.
Where is Colégio Luso-Francês located?
Colégio Luso-Francês is located at Rua do Amial, 442, 4200-054 Porto, in the north of the city. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
