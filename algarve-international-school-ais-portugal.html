Algarve International School (AIS) is an international school located in Algarve founded in 2019. The school follows the UK national Cambridge curriculum, with English as the primary language of instruction.

transparent culture, small classes, non-selective school

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. with approximately 260 students. Additional languages offered include Spanish, Portuguese, French, German.
