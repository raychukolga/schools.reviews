Colégio Mira Rio is a private Portuguese school in Telheiras, in the north of Lisbon, founded in 1978 by a group of families. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, and in secondary also offers the IB Diploma (IB World School since 2018).

The nursery and pre-school are mixed; from Grade 1 the school is for girls only, and boys continue at its sister school, Colégio Planalto. Both belong to Colégios Fomento, a cooperative that runs four schools in Lisbon and Porto. Its educational project is based on a Christian view of the person, with a personal tutor for every pupil. In 2017 the school moved from Restelo to new buildings in Telheiras.

* Fees are set jointly for the Colégios Fomento in Lisbon. Tuition is paid monthly: 10 months a year, 11 for the nursery and pre-school. Enrollment is 10% lower if paid by 31 May. Sibling discount on tuition and enrollment: 5% for 2 children up to 35% for 6 or more; 2% off for paying the year upfront by 31 July. Scholarships are available through the Fomento scholarship fund. Always verify fees directly with the school — they change annually.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Mira Rio follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese; in secondary pupils can also take the IB Diploma.
How much does Colégio Mira Rio cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,540 (Grades 1–4) to €8,460 (secondary / IB), paid monthly, plus an application fee of €300 for new pupils and an annual enrollment fee of €228–€471.
Is Colégio Mira Rio a girls-only school?
The nursery and pre-school are mixed. From Grade 1 to Grade 12 the school is for girls only; boys continue at Colégio Planalto, also in Telheiras.
