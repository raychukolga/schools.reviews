Colégio Pedro Arrupe is a private Portuguese school in Parque das Nações, Lisbon. It was created by the Alves Ribeiro group, which invited the Portuguese Jesuits to collaborate; the first stone was laid in 2009. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, with Ignatian pedagogy.

The campus was built with sustainable materials such as cork and glass bricks and has green roofs, two meadows, an orchard, a vegetable garden, three outdoor amphitheatres, two libraries, science and computer labs, a music school, a chapel and a 500-seat auditorium.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Pedro Arrupe follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, with Ignatian pedagogy.
How much does Colégio Pedro Arrupe cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,800 (pre-school) to €7,050 (secondary) a year, plus enrollment (€560 for new pupils, €460 renewal). Meals (€152 a month) are compulsory in pre-school and Grades 1–4.
Is Colégio Pedro Arrupe a Jesuit school?
It belongs to the Alves Ribeiro group, not to the Jesuits, but was created in collaboration with them and follows Ignatian pedagogy.
