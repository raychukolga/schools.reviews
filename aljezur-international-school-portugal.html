Aljezur International School is an international school located in Aljezur founded in 2010. The school follows the Cambridge IGCSE curriculum, with English as the primary language of instruction.

Converted farmhouse — the school is housed in an impressive converted farmhouse 5 km south of Aljezur. aljezur-international 5 continents — they have taught children from five continents over the years. aljezur-international Only open Cambridge centre in the Algarve — they accept external students of all ages and backgrounds to sit Cambridge exams, not just their own enrolled students. aljezur-int

The school accepts students from age 11 to 18. Additional languages offered include Portuguese, German, Spanish.

Sport & activities within curriculum
Sport
We provide a diverse experience of Physical Education. In the past students have followed an holistic theme and learned basic yoga and martial art movements. Students have also been introduced to Tai Chi and Capoeira. For weekly sports lessons we usually use the municipal sports centre in Aljezur and the adjoining swimming pool. with students alternating between team games, gymnastics and swimming. Our sports instructors implement a complete individual fitness programme with students’ fitness levels being monitored closely and progress being recorded. Last year students were also instructed in various water sports at the beach as well as being put through their paces in cross country running round our land.
