Vilamoura International School (CIV) is an international school located in Vilamoura founded in 1984. The school follows the International cirriculum (British), National cirriculum (Portuguese) curriculum, with Portuguese — compulsory for all students English — main language of instruction on the international pathway French — taught to all students as the primary language of instruction.

Top ranked — ranked #1 school in the Algarve and #4 in Portugal. civ - 900 students, 45 nationalities — approximately 60% Portuguese and 40% international, representing 45 nationalities. civ - Up to 5 languages — Portuguese, English, French, Spanish, and Russian all available on campus. - National champions — one student became a national golf champion and another a karate champion. aljezur-intern

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. Additional languages offered include Spanish .
