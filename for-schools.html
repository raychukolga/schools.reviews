Listing

At this stage, everything is free for schools.

Every school on Schools Reviews is listed free of charge: the school profile page, the place in the catalogue and on the map, and inclusion in the comparison tool. A school does not pay to be listed or to be reviewed.

Our Goal

Verified information and independent reviews.

Our goal is to give families verified information about schools — fees, programmes, ages, languages, accreditations — in one place, together with an independent review written from a parent’s perspective.

✓
Verified information
We check school data against official sources and mark profiles we have verified with the month of the check.
★
Independent reviews
Reviews are written by our editorial team, not by the school, and are not paid for.
◎
Neutral and objective
We describe each school as it is — facts first, without ratings or rankings.
→
The right fit
We help families understand which schools suit them, so they visit the right ones.
Our Approach

Different schools for different families.

Our aim is not to find something bad about a school. It is to show that different schools suit different families.
Each review has two parts: “What we like” and “Worth noting”. Worth noting is not criticism — it is what a family should know to decide whether the school fits them: the religious ethos, the language balance, extra costs, the admissions process, and so on.
Corrections

Something out of date? Tell us.

If your school’s information needs updating — new fees, a new programme, a new address or anything else — please write to editor@schools.reviews. It helps if you include:

  • the school name and a link to its page on Schools Reviews;
  • what should be changed;
  • a source we can check — for example the fee schedule or a page on the school’s website.
Coming Later

Paid options for more visibility.

In the future we will add optional paid features that help schools increase their visibility on Schools Reviews. Listing will stay free, and paid options will not change what we write about a school.
