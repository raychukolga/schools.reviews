Colégio Novo da Maia is a private Portuguese school in Milheirós, Maia, just north of Porto. It opened on 12 September 2001 with about thirty children in the nursery and pre-school, and now has three buildings covering the nursery, pre-school, basic and secondary education.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum with a wide “complementary offer”: daily English from the nursery, CLIL and Cambridge English, STEAM, philosophy, theatre, financial literacy, project work (Projeto A+) and a socio-emotional programme. In secondary it adds exam preparation, split lab classes, Model UN and an entrepreneurship incubator.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Novo da Maia cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €5,115 (pre-school) to €6,732 (baby room, meals included) a year, paid in 11 instalments, plus enrollment (€360) and meals.
How good are Colégio Novo da Maia’s results?
According to the school it ranked 1st among basic schools and 6th in secondary in the 2023/24 national rankings.
Does Colégio Novo da Maia have a school bus?
Yes, within the municipality of Maia, at €233–€290 a month.
