Colégio de Gaia was founded in 1933 on the Quinta do Trancoso in Mafamude, Vila Nova de Gaia, on the initiative of the Bishop of Porto; it received its licence in 1934. It is owned by the Diocese of Porto and expanded greatly in the 1960s and 1970s.

Today it teaches about 1,500 students from pre-school to Grade 12 on the Portuguese national curriculum. In secondary it runs 13 courses with its own study plans in areas such as engineering, health, IT and multimedia, sport and management, which give access to university and a professional qualification. Families in pre-school and basic education can apply for state support (contrato de desenvolvimento).

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio de Gaia cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — contact geral@colgaia.pt or the secretariat (223 754 007).
What does Colégio de Gaia offer in secondary?
13 courses with the school’s own study plans in areas such as engineering, health, IT, sport and management, giving access to university and a level-4 professional qualification.
Who runs Colégio de Gaia?
The Diocese of Porto; it is a Catholic school founded in 1933.
