Salesianos de Lisboa — the Oficinas de São José — is a private Catholic school run by the Salesians of Don Bosco in Estrela, Lisbon. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from Grade 1 to Grade 12.

The Salesians began their work at the Oficinas de São José in the 1890s, and the current building opened in 1906. Once a trades school with boarding, it has been a regular academic school since the 1970s. Its educational project follows the style of Don Bosco, based on Christian humanism.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Salesianos de Lisboa follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from Grade 1 to Grade 12.
How much does Salesianos de Lisboa cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — ask the school office. Families can apply for income-based state support, and top students in Grades 10–12 can receive merit scholarships.
Is Salesianos de Lisboa a religious school?
Yes. It is a Catholic school run by the Salesians of Don Bosco.
