Colégio do Sagrado Coração de Maria is a private Catholic school in Arroios, central Lisbon, belonging to the Religious of the Sacred Heart of Mary. It opened in 1941 and follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12.

In 1991 the Ministry of Education awarded it the Diploma of Pedagogical Merit. The school has playing fields, gyms, laboratories and conference rooms, and runs projects such as Philosophy for Children and STEAM.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio do Sagrado Coração de Maria follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12.
How much does it cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,880 (pre-school) to €7,180 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 monthly instalments, plus an annual enrollment fee of €515 that includes insurance and school trips.
Is it a religious school?
Yes. It is a Catholic school of the Religious of the Sacred Heart of Mary.
