Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does King's College School Lisbon follow?
King's College School Lisbon follows the Cambridge/ IB/ A-Level curriculum. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does King's College School Lisbon cost per year?
Annual tuition at King's College School Cascais for 2026/27 ranges from €12,500 (Early Years) to €27,500 (Years 12–13 / IB / A-Level). The one-time enrolment fee is €3,000 for EY1–Y6 or €5,250 for Y7–Y13, plus a €250 registration fee per family. Annual re-enrolment is €600. Boarding is an additional €21,950–€25,900/year. Always verify fees directly with the school.
What ages does King's College School Lisbon accept?
King's College School Lisbon accepts students from age 3 to 18. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where is King's College School Lisbon located?
King's College School Lisbon is located at R. Doctor Manuel Antonio Costa Matos 164, 2750-290 Cascais. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
Does King's College School Lisbon offer boarding?
Yes, King's College School Cascais offers full boarding (€25,900/yr) and weekly boarding Monday–Friday (€21,950/yr), both additional to tuition. Boarding includes accommodation, all meals, laundry, sports access, guided study, Wi-Fi and weekend activities. Mandatory health insurance (€1,000/yr) is also required for boarders.
