Deutsche Schule Porto is an international school located in Porto founded in 1901. The school follows the German International Abitur (DIA) curriculum, with German/Portuguese as the primary language of instruction.

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. with approximately 800 students. Additional languages offered include English, French.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Deutsche Schule Porto follow?
Deutsche Schule Porto follows the German International Abitur (DIA) curriculum. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does Deutsche Schule Porto cost per year?
Annual tuition at Deutsche Schule Porto ranges from €5,000 to €9,000 per year. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €620. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these can change annually.
What ages does Deutsche Schule Porto accept?
Deutsche Schule Porto accepts students from age 3 to 18. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
