Vale Verde International School (VVIS) is a British international school on the Estrada do Burgau, between Luz and Burgau near Lagos, in the Western Algarve. It was licensed by the Portuguese Ministry of Education in 2002 and opened a bilingual primary school in 2010.

Primary pupils (Years 1–6) follow the UK National Curriculum, with literacy, numeracy and science in English and subjects such as PE, drama, music, arts and Portuguese culture taught in Portuguese. Secondary students follow Cambridge Lower Secondary (Years 7–9), then Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel IGCSEs (Years 10–11) and AS and A Levels (Years 12–13). The school reports 27 nationalities.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Vale Verde International School follow?
A British pathway: UK National Curriculum in a bilingual primary, Cambridge Lower Secondary, then Cambridge or Pearson Edexcel IGCSEs and AS/A Levels.
How much does Vale Verde International School cost per year?
For 2026/27, tuition is €7,623 – €13,365 a year, plus a €500 registration fee, €500 refundable deposit, €150 annual re-enrolment and €50 insurance.
What ages does the school take?
Students from 5 to 18 (Year 1 to Year 13). There is no nursery or kindergarten.
Is there a school bus?
Yes, from Portimão, at €550 per term with a full-year commitment.
