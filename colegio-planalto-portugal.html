Colégio Planalto is a private Portuguese school in Telheiras, in the north of Lisbon, founded in 1978 by a group of parents and teachers. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, and in the secondary years offers the IB Diploma — it was the first school in Portugal to adopt it (IB World School since 1994, first diplomas in 1996).

It takes children from the nursery to the end of secondary school. The nursery and pre-school are mixed; from Grade 1 the school is for boys only, and girls continue at its sister school, Colégio Mira Rio. Both belong to Colégios Fomento, a cooperative that runs four schools in Lisbon and Porto. The educational project is based on a Christian view of the person, with a personal tutor for every pupil.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Planalto follow?
Colégio Planalto follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, and offers the IB Diploma Programme in the secondary years.
How much does Colégio Planalto cost per year?
Annual tuition for 2026/27 ranges from €5,540 (Grades 1–4) to €8,460 (IB Diploma), paid monthly. New pupils pay an application fee of €300, and every year there is an enrollment fee of €228–€471. Always verify current fees directly with the school.
Is Colégio Planalto a boys-only school?
The nursery and pre-school are mixed. From Grade 1 to the end of secondary school, Colégio Planalto is for boys only; girls continue at its sister school, Colégio Mira Rio, also in Telheiras.
