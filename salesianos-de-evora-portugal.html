Salesianos de Évora is a Catholic private school run by the Salesians of Don Bosco (Fundação Salesianos). The Salesians came to Évora in 1926 at the invitation of the archbishop to care for the city's neglected young people, and the school now sits on Avenida São João Bosco.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from nursery to Grade 12, with Sciences & Technology, Socio-economics and Languages & Humanities in secondary. Next to the school it runs ArtiSport, a sports and performing-arts school, a parish and youth groups.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Salesianos de Évora cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — ask the school office. Fees are paid in 9 monthly instalments, September to May.
Does the school offer scholarships?
Yes, for Grades 10–12: 100%, 30% or 15% off the monthly fees for pupils with top internal marks and national exam results.
Do pupils get Cambridge English certificates?
English lessons include Cambridge preparation, and in their last year of English every pupil takes a Cambridge exam free of charge.
