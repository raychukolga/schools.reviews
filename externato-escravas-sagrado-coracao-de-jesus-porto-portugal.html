The Externato is run by the Handmaids of the Sacred Heart of Jesus (Escravas do Sagrado Coração de Jesus), a Catholic congregation. The sisters began teaching in Porto in 1940, and the school has been in its building on Rua Carlos Malheiro Dias since 1957.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from pre-school to Grade 9, following the congregation's "Pedagogy of the Heart".

Frequently asked questions
How much does the Externato das Escravas cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — ask the school office. Families may get state support through contratos simples or de desenvolvimento, depending on income.
Up to which grade does the school go?
From pre-school up to Grade 9.
