Aprendizes (“learners”), officially Escola Os Aprendizes, is a private, non-religious school in Cobre, on the green edge of Cascais below the Sintra hills. It opened in 2007 with pre-school and primary, added Grades 5–6 in 2014 and now runs from age 3 to 18. It calls itself an “International Active Learning School”, with the motto “Together we Educate for Change”. The school reports around 500 students from 38 nationalities; most families live locally and plan to stay in Portugal.

Pre-school (mixed ages 3–6) and primary (Grades 1–4) follow an enriched Portuguese national curriculum, with Portuguese as the main language and English every day. From Grade 5 students switch to the Cambridge International curriculum taught in English, sitting IGCSEs in Grades 9–10 and AS and A Levels — or the AICE Diploma — in Grades 11–12. Lessons are active and student-centred, with yoga, theatre, workshops, gardening and a social-emotional programme (SER) for all ages. Graduates have gone on to universities in Portugal, Spain, the UK, the Netherlands, Brazil and the USA.

Frequently asked questions
Is Aprendizes an English-speaking school?
Partly. In pre-school and primary (up to Grade 4) Portuguese is the main language, with English taught every day. From Grade 5 the school follows the Cambridge curriculum in English.
How much does Aprendizes cost?
For 2026/27, tuition is €10,923 – €17,589 a year depending on grade, with meals included. New students also pay an admission fee (two monthly fees) and every family pays an annual registration fee (one monthly fee), plus insurance, materials and Cambridge exam fees.
Does Aprendizes offer the IB?
No. The school follows the Cambridge pathway — IGCSE, then AS and A Levels or the AICE Diploma. To compare the two systems, see our guide IB vs Cambridge.
How do admissions work?
Families pre-register by email, visit the school (about an hour, or an online interview), send previous school reports and may be invited to a trial day. Students joining from Grade 7 take an English test. Admissions open in the autumn before the start year; the school is currently full, so places are not guaranteed.
