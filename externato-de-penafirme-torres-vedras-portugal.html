Externato de Penafirme is a Catholic school in Póvoa de Penafirme, near A dos Cunhados, about 15 km from Torres Vedras. It opened on 3 November 1975 as the successor of the Penafirme minor seminary (1960), in buildings next to an 18th-century Augustinian convent, and has taught more than ten thousand pupils.

It teaches Grades 5–12 on the Portuguese national curriculum, with academic secondary courses and, since 2005, the Penafirme Vocational School (health, renewable energy, management, multimedia, catering, electromechanics). The school is part of the local public school network through a state contract, so contract places are free for pupils from its area.

Frequently asked questions
Is Externato de Penafirme free?
Yes for pupils in its state-funded contract classes, who pay the same as in a public school. These places are only for pupils living in the area covered by the contract.
What does Externato de Penafirme teach?
The Portuguese national curriculum from Grade 5 to Grade 12, with academic secondary tracks and vocational courses.
Where is Externato de Penafirme?
In Póvoa de Penafirme, A dos Cunhados, about 15 km from Torres Vedras near the coast.
