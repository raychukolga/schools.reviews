Colégio Quinta do Lago is a private Portuguese school in São Domingos de Rana, Cascais, founded in 1988 by Maria Luísa Lagoa on a former farm estate. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from the nursery to Grade 9.

The school has grown to three buildings and runs citizenship projects with the local community. Admission follows its internal rules, with the order of pre-registration as one of the criteria.
