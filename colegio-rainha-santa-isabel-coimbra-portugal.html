Colégio Rainha Santa Isabel (CRSI) is a Catholic private school in central Coimbra. It was founded in 1900, taken over by the Congregation of the Sisters of St Joseph of Cluny in 1928 and has been in its current building on Rua do Brasil since 1941.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from pre-school to Grade 12 and offers every science and humanities track in secondary. Teaching follows the Cluny pedagogy, with a yearly cultural theme across all levels, a strong arts programme and Cambridge English exam classes.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Rainha Santa Isabel cost?
In 2025/26 tuition is €3,350 a year (pre-school and Grades 1–4) to €4,560 (secondary), paid in 10 instalments, plus €200 enrollment and meals.
Can families get state support?
Yes. Pre-school can be covered by a contrato de desenvolvimento and Grades 1–4 and secondary by a contrato simples — both depend on family income and are applied for through the Ministry of Education.
Does the school teach English?
English is included in pre-school. From Grade 1 the school runs Cambridge English exam classes as a paid extra (€55–€95 a month), plus French, Spanish and German certificate courses.
