Santo António International School (SAIS) is an independent Catholic school in Monte Estoril, between Cascais and Estoril. It opened in September 2013 and has followed the Cambridge International programme since the start. Around 280 students from 49 nationalities attend, from toddlers aged 2 to sixth-formers; Portuguese families are the largest group.

Early Years and Primary follow the English National Curriculum with Cambridge Primary, Middle School leads to Cambridge IGCSEs in Years 10–11, and the final two years lead to A Levels and the Cambridge AICE Diploma. Teaching is in English, with Portuguese in the curriculum and Spanish, French and Mandarin as further languages. From 2026/27 the school runs on three campuses in Estoril: Campus I (Rua Dr. António Martins) for ages 2–11, Campus II (Avenida Sabóia) for Middle School and Campus III (Rua de Nice) for ages 16–19. Graduates go on to universities in the UK, the USA, Portugal and elsewhere in Europe.

Frequently asked questions
How much does SAIS cost?
For 2026/27, tuition is €10,950 – €14,700 a year depending on age. Families also pay a €1,700 admission fee each year and €250 insurance; new families pay a €1,250 deposit and a €1,350 reservation fee. Meals (€250 a month) and Cambridge exam fees are extra.
Is SAIS a Catholic school?
Yes. SAIS is an independent Catholic school; religious education reflects that identity and also covers world religions. Families of all faiths and none are welcome.
Does SAIS offer the IB?
No. SAIS follows the Cambridge pathway — IGCSE, then A Levels and the AICE Diploma. To compare the two systems, see our guide IB vs Cambridge.
How do admissions work?
Families fill in a pre-enrolment form, then visit the school for an interview with the head or a coordinator and send recent school reports. Tests in English, maths, science and Portuguese may be required. Applications are accepted all year, subject to places; there is a waiting list.
