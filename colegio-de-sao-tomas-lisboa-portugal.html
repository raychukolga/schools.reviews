Colégio de São Tomás is a private Catholic school in Lisbon, founded in 2003 by the Associação para a Educação, Cultura e Formação (APECEF). It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese.

It has two campuses: Quinta das Conchas in Lumiar, from pre-school to the end of secondary school, and Sete Rios, for pre-school and Grades 1–4. Together the school community counts about 1,600 people.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio de São Tomás follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese.
How much does Colégio de São Tomás cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,805 (pre-school) to €7,165 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 monthly instalments, plus an annual enrollment fee equal to one month’s tuition.
Where are the campuses?
Quinta das Conchas (Av. Maria Helena Vieira da Silva 39, Lumiar) for pre-school to Grade 12, and Sete Rios (R. Prof. Lima Basto 133) for pre-school and Grades 1–4.
