Montessori Bright Academy (MBA) is a small international Montessori school founded by Fabienne Mikou. It sits in a traditional Portuguese building on Avenida Almirante Gago Coutinho in Alvalade, with direct access to woodland, and has around 70 children from 37 nationalities.

Children are grouped in mixed-age Montessori communities: Toddler (1–3), Primary or Children's House (3–6) and Elementary (6–12). English is the main language of instruction, with regular Portuguese from age one.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Montessori Bright Academy cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €2,950 a term for toddlers and €3,115 a term for primary and elementary, or €8,410 and €8,880 if paid for the whole year in advance. Meals are €1,300 a year and the enrolment fee is €2,080–2,580 a year.
What language do children learn in?
English is the main language. Children also hear Portuguese every day from age one.
Up to what age does the school go?
From the moment a child can walk (about age 1) to age 12.
