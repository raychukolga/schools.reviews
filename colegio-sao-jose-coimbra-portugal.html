Colégio de São José is a Catholic private school in Montes Claros, Coimbra, run by Colégio São José, Lda. Its teaching is based on the Schoenstatt pedagogy of Joseph Kentenich and on its own model, VOAR: attachment, boldness, autonomy and responsibility.

It holds a Ministry of Education licence (alvará no. 992) for pre-school and basic education in the Portuguese curriculum, and runs an international section with the Clonlara School programme from Ann Arbor, Michigan, for Grades 1 to 12. It also offers a homeschooling option and takes part in Erasmus+.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio de São José cost?
The school does not publish its tuition online. Registration is €160 (non-refundable); ask the school office for the 2026/27 price list.
What is the Clonlara programme?
An international programme from Clonlara School in Ann Arbor, Michigan (USA), taught at the school from Grade 1 to Grade 12 in small multi-age groups, with an individual learning plan for each pupil.
Does the Portuguese track go up to Grade 12?
No. The Portuguese national curriculum runs from kindergarten to Grade 9; Grades 10–12 are only offered in the Clonlara programme.
