Colégio Horizonte is a private Portuguese girls’ school in Vila Nova de Gaia, by the Arrábida bridge across the Douro from Porto. It opened on 8 September 1979 and belongs to the Colégios Fomento, alongside the boys’ school Cedros next door and Planalto and Mira Rio in Lisbon.

It runs the mixed nursery and pre-school for both schools (Optimist project) and then teaches girls from Grade 1 to Grade 12 on the Portuguese national curriculum, with Singapore maths in primary, a Christian-inspired formation programme and a personal tutor for every pupil.

Frequently asked questions
Is Colégio Horizonte a girls’ school?
Yes, from Grade 1. The nursery and pre-school are mixed; boys continue at Colégio Cedros next door.
How much does Colégio Horizonte cost?
In 2025/26 tuition was €4,752 (pre-school) to €6,580 (secondary) a year, plus registration and enrollment; 2026/27 fees are not yet published.
Where is Colégio Horizonte?
In Vila Nova de Gaia, by the Arrábida bridge; school buses run from central Porto.
