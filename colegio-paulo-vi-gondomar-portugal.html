Colégio Paulo VI is a private Portuguese school in São Cosme, Gondomar, just east of Porto. It was founded in 1964 and for its first 30 years was run by the Capuchin friars as a small secondary school; since 1994 it has grown into a large campus covering every level from the nursery to Grade 12.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum with its own additions: philosophy for children and young people, coding and robotics, project-based learning and English from pre-school. Since 2019/20 it offers an integrated arts profile in music and dance, and secondary students can apply for merit and need-based scholarships.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Paulo VI cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is about €4,144 (pre-school) to €5,642 (baby nursery, meals included) a year, plus enrollment and meals.
Does Colégio Paulo VI offer scholarships?
Yes, for secondary: in 2026/27 two 100% and eight 50% scholarships, awarded on national exam results and family income.
Does Colégio Paulo VI have a school bus?
Yes, its own buses across Gondomar and nearby areas, €57.75–€178.65 a month depending on the zone.
