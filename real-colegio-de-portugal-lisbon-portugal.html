Real Colégio de Portugal opened on 1 September 1999 in the Palácio do Conde do Lumiar, a former country house in the historic Paço do Lumiar quarter of Lisbon. It also uses the nearby Quinta do Pisani.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from nursery to Grade 12. Through Academica, pupils in Grades 8–11 can follow the US Dual Diploma online and graduate with an American High School diploma as well.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Real Colégio de Portugal cost?
In 2026/27 from €476 a month in nursery to €554 a month in secondary, paid over 11 months (€5,236–6,094 a year). Meals and transport are extra.
What is the Dual Diploma?
An online US High School programme run with Academica. Pupils in Grades 8–11 take American credits alongside their Portuguese studies and graduate with both diplomas.
Is there a school bus?
Yes — one-way or door-to-door transport for a monthly fee.
