Carlucci American International School — known as CAISL — is the only US State Department-sponsored school in Portugal, and the only international school in the country offering the American High School Diploma. For families with children planning to study at US universities, this is a significant differentiator.

The campus in Linhó, Sintra is one of the largest and best-equipped of any international school in the Lisbon area — a library with 40,000 books, a 450-seat theatre, large sports fields, and dedicated arts and music facilities. The school is non-profit: all tuition fees are reinvested directly into education.

One of CAISL's strongest points is how much is included in the tuition. Sport — basketball, soccer, swimming, volleyball, cross country, track and field, softball — is part of the curriculum, not offered as paid extras. The after-school activity offering on top of that is equally broad, covering music, arts, coding, and more.

Frequently asked questions
Is CAISL the only American school in Portugal?+
Yes — CAISL is the only US State Department-sponsored school in Portugal and the only school offering the American High School Diploma. It is the natural choice for families planning a return to the US or targeting American universities.
What is included in the CAISL tuition?+
Sport is embedded in the curriculum — basketball, soccer, swimming, volleyball, cross country, track and field, and softball are all part of the programme, not paid extras. This is relatively unusual among international schools in Portugal. Lunch is not included and costs approximately €1,212/yr optionally.
How much does CAISL cost per year?+
Annual tuition ranges from €12,128 (Kindergarten) to €23,532 (Grades 9–12). There is a one-time enrollment fee of €600 and an annual re-enrollment fee of €1,935 for Grades 1–5. Always verify current fees directly with the school.
Where exactly is CAISL located?+
CAISL is in Linhó, in the municipality of Sintra — approximately 30 minutes from central Lisbon by car. The school runs its own bus network covering the main residential areas of the Lisbon metro area, including Cascais, Oeiras, and Lisbon itself.
Does CAISL offer the IB Diploma?+
Yes — in addition to the American High School Diploma, CAISL offers the IB Diploma at the senior level. The school's IB average is 34.3 out of 45, above the global average of 30.2.
