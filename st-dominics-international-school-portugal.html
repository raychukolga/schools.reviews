St Dominic's International School is one of the few schools in Portugal to offer the full International Baccalaureate continuum — Primary Years Programme (PYP), Middle Years Programme (MYP), and IB Diploma (DP) — all under one roof. For families committed to the IB pathway from the start, this removes the risk of a curriculum change mid-schooling.

Founded in 1988 and located in São Domingos de Rana — between Cascais and Lisbon — the school has over 35 years of history in Portugal. It is genuinely international: more than 50 nationalities are represented in the student body. The campus sits within 7,000m² of private gardens and outdoor space.

Teaching is in English, with Portuguese offered as an additional language. Class sizes are kept small — between 16 and 20 students — and the school runs its own transport network across the Lisbon area.

Frequently asked questions
Does St Dominic's offer the full IB continuum?
Yes — St Dominic's is one of the few schools in Portugal to offer the complete IB continuum: Primary Years Programme (PYP), Middle Years Programme (MYP), and IB Diploma (DP) in sequence. This means children can follow a single, uninterrupted IB pathway from age 3 through to graduation — with no need to change schools or curricula at any point.
What are St Dominic's IB results?
The IB Diploma average score was 32 out of 45 in 2024, above the global average of 30.2. Results for 2025 have not been published. Always verify the most recent results directly with the school.
How much does St Dominic's cost per year?
Annual tuition ranges from €11,778 (Nursery) to €23,166 (Grades 11–12). There is a one-time enrollment fee of €5,250 and an annual re-enrollment fee of €1,178–€2,316 depending on the year group. Lunch is not included and costs approximately €1,450/yr. Always verify current fees directly with the school.
Where is St Dominic's located?
The school is in São Domingos de Rana, in the municipality of Cascais — approximately 25 minutes from central Lisbon and 10 minutes from Cascais. The school runs its own bus network serving the main residential areas of the Lisbon metro.
What is the school community like?
St Dominic's has a highly international student body with more than 50 nationalities represented. Class sizes are kept small — between 16 and 20 students — and the campus includes 7,000m² of private gardens and outdoor space.
