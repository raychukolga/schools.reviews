St Dominic's International School is an international school located in São Domingos de Rana founded in 1997. The school follows the IB curriculum, with English as the primary language of instruction.

7000 sqm of private gardens and wooded areas to enjoy the outdoors

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. Additional languages offered include Portuguese.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does St Dominic's International School follow?
St Dominic's International School follows the IB curriculum. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does St Dominic's International School cost per year?
Annual tuition at St Dominic's International School ranges from €11,778 to €23,166 per year. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €5,250. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these can change annually.
What ages does St Dominic's International School accept?
St Dominic's International School accepts students from age 3 to 18. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where is St Dominic's International School located?
St Dominic's International School is located at R. Maria Brown 311A, 2785-816 São Domingos de Rana. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
