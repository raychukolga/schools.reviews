Colégio Efanor is a private Portuguese school in Senhora da Hora, Matosinhos, just north of Porto. It is owned by the Belmiro de Azevedo Foundation, a non-profit set up by the founder of Sonae, and opened in 2008/09 in the former social building of the Efanor factory; it has run up to Grade 12 since 2018/19.

It has two sites on the same avenue: Site 1 for the nursery, pre-school and Grades 1–4, and Site 2 for Grades 5–12. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum with a “local curriculum” of compulsory extra subjects — English, Spanish, CLIL, arts, swimming, chess, computer science — included in the fee.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Efanor cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €6,620 (Grades 5–9) to €7,800 (nursery) a year, plus enrollment (€540 for new pupils) and lunch from pre-school.
Who runs Colégio Efanor?
The Belmiro de Azevedo Foundation, a non-profit foundation; the school is open to all families, not only Efanor/Sonae employees.
What curriculum does Colégio Efanor follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, with extra compulsory subjects such as English, Spanish, swimming, chess and computer science.
