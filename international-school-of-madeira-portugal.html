The International School of Madeira (ISM) is an English-medium Cambridge primary school in Funchal, founded in 1997 and run by teachers. It is a registered Cambridge International School and describes itself as the only Cambridge-accredited international primary school on the island.

Children join at 3 in the Infant School (Nursery, Pre-School and Reception) and continue through Cambridge Primary to Grade 4 (age 10). The core subjects are English, Mathematics and Science, with Cambridge Global Perspectives; Portuguese is taught as a separate subject from Grade 1. The school is at Quinta Martins, with large gardens and a library.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does the International School of Madeira follow?
ISM follows the Cambridge Early Years and Cambridge Primary curricula, taught in English, with Portuguese as a separate subject from Grade 1.
How much does the International School of Madeira cost per year?
For 2026/27, tuition is €5,520 – €6,240 a year, plus a €350 annual administration charge and €80 re-enrolment. Lunch is €112 a month.
What ages does the school take?
Children from 3 to 10 — Nursery to Grade 4. The school has no secondary section.
Where is the International School of Madeira located?
At Quinta Martins, Rua da Levada de Santa Luzia 40, 9050-219 Funchal.
