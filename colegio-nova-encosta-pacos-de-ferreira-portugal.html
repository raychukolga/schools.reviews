Colégio Nova Encosta is a private Portuguese school in Paços de Ferreira, north-east of Porto. It opened in 2011 as an offshoot of the “Encosta dos Sonhos” nursery project and added secondary education in 2013/14, so it now covers all compulsory schooling from Grade 1 to Grade 12.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum in a purpose-built three-storey building, with Cambridge English exams, Eco-Schools and Erasmus+ projects, and an unusually broad in-house support team including psychology and therapies.
