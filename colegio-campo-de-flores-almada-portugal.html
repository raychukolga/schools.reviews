Colégio Campo de Flores is a private Portuguese school in Lazarim, Caparica, in the municipality of Almada. It opened in 1967 with 150 pupils, founded by Maria Helena and Ricardo de Almeida, and is still run by their family; today it has more than 1,370 pupils.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12. The name honours the poet and educator João de Deus, whose reading method inspired the founder. Secondary students can add the American high school diploma online through the Dual Diploma programme (Academica International Studies).

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Campo de Flores follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, with an optional online US Dual Diploma in secondary.
How much does Colégio Campo de Flores cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €4,680 (pre-school and Grades 1–4) to €5,480 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 instalments, plus registration (€265) and meals.
Does Colégio Campo de Flores have a school bus?
Yes — buses cover Caparica, Almada, Seixal, Fernão Ferro and Azeitão, at €105–€150 a month depending on the zone.
