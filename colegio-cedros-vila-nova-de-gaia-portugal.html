Colégio Cedros is a private Portuguese boys’ school in Vila Nova de Gaia, at the southern end of the Arrábida bridge across the Douro from Porto. It opened in October 1979, founded by parents and teachers inspired by St Josemaría Escrivá, and has been on its current site since 1983. It belongs to the Colégios Fomento, which also run Planalto and Mira Rio in Lisbon and the girls’ school Horizonte next door.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum with single-sex education from Grade 1 and a personal tutor for every pupil. Primary uses the Keep Project with Singapore maths; secondary runs the Smart Choices programme, with flexible subject choices, work placements and a volunteering project.

Frequently asked questions
Is Colégio Cedros a boys’ school?
Yes, from Grade 1. The nursery and pre-school are mixed and run at Colégio Horizonte next door; girls continue at Horizonte.
How much does Colégio Cedros cost?
In 2025/26 tuition was €4,810 (Grades 1–4) to €6,580 (secondary) a year, plus registration and enrollment; 2026/27 fees are not yet published.
Where is Colégio Cedros?
In Vila Nova de Gaia, at the southern end of the Arrábida bridge; school buses run from central Porto.
