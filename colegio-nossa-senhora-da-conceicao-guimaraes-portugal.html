Colégio de Nossa Senhora da Conceição is a non-profit Catholic school owned by the Irmandade de Nossa Senhora da Consolação e Santos Passos in Guimarães. Its first primary class opened in 1877, and the Franciscan sisters shaped its values for many years.

Today it teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from nursery to Grade 9. It is a Cambridge English Educational Partner and offers an integrated music course with the Guimarães Conservatoire.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio de Nossa Senhora da Conceição cost?
In 2026/27 €396 a month for pre-school (lunch included), €325 for Grades 1–4 and €390 for Grades 5–9, paid over 11 months. Enrolment is €120.
Does the school teach music?
Yes — it runs an integrated music course with the Guimarães Conservatoire, and instrument lessons are available as an extracurricular activity.
Up to which grade does the school go?
Up to Grade 9.
