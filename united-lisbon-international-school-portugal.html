United Lisbon International School is an international school in Park of Nations, Lisbon, founded in 2021. The school follows the IEYC and IPC curricula in early and primary years, and the IB Diploma Programme in senior school, with English as the primary language of instruction.

The campus is modern and well-equipped, with 6,500 m² of outdoor spaces and play areas. The school accepts students from age 3 to 18, with approximately 600 students. Additional languages offered include Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, and Mandarin.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does United Lisbon International School follow?
United Lisbon follows the IEYC (International Early Years Curriculum) in early years, IPC (International Primary Curriculum) in primary school, and the IB Diploma Programme in senior school. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does United Lisbon International School cost per year?
Annual tuition at United Lisbon International School ranges from €11,800 to €24,000 per year. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €3,750 and an application fee of €310. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these can change annually.
What ages does United Lisbon International School accept?
United Lisbon International School accepts students from age 3 to 18. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where is United Lisbon International School located?
United Lisbon International School is located at Av. Mar. Gomes da Costa No 9, 1800-255 Lisboa. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
