CLIP — Oporto International School (formerly Colégio Luso-Internacional do Porto) is an international school located in Porto founded in 1990. The school follows the British Curriculum (National Curriculum for England), with English/ Portuguese as the primary language of instruction.

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. with approximately 1200 students. Additional languages offered include Spanish, French, German, Mandarin.
