St James' Primary School is a bilingual primary school in Cobre, Cascais, for children aged 6 to 10. It was opened in 2007 as an extension of St. George's School, which has been in Cascais since 1960, and follows the same guidelines and ethos. The school was inaugurated by the deputy mayor of Cascais and the director of the British Council in Lisbon.

Pupils spend their time equally between English and Portuguese and between the two curricula, supported by the English National Curriculum. The school follows a classical model with arts at its heart, aiming to prepare children to continue in either Portuguese or international schools.

After primary, pupils can continue next door at St. George's School (Years 6–13), a bilingual Cambridge school that works in partnership with St James' and shares its curriculum, through to IGCSE and A Levels.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does St James' Primary School Cascais follow?
St James' teaches bilingually: time is split equally between English and Portuguese and between the English National Curriculum and the Portuguese curriculum.
How much does St James' Primary School Cascais cost per year?
Annual tuition at St James' Primary School Cascais is €7,650 per year. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €800. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these can change annually.
What ages does St James' Primary School Cascais accept?
St James' Primary School Cascais accepts students from age 6 to 10. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where do pupils go after St James'?
They can continue next door at St. George's School, its partner school for Years 6–13, with Cambridge IGCSE and A Levels.
Where is St James' Primary School Cascais located?
St James' Primary School Cascais is located at R. Depósitos da Água 339 1º, 2750-561 Cascais. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
