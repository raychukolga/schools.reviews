CLIB — Colégio Luso Internacional de Braga, also called The Braga International School — is a private, co-educational, non-denominational school in Gualtar, on the eastern edge of Braga near the University of Minho. It was founded in 1999; co-founder Helena Pina Vaz is still the principal. The school has more than 370 students aged 3 to 18 from over 50 nationalities, and has been owned since 2024 by Dukes Education, a UK group with schools across Europe, including CLIP in Porto.

Teaching is in English throughout. The Infant Department (ages 3–5) follows Cambridge Early Years, the Lower School (Reception to Form 4) Cambridge Primary, and the Upper School Cambridge Lower Secondary, then IGCSEs and the Cambridge AICE Diploma with AS and A Levels. Portuguese language, history and culture are taught alongside, and the C.A.S.E. programme (Creative, Action, Service, Enrichment) counts towards the final graduation mark. The campus is closed and green, with two buildings joined by a glass corridor.

Frequently asked questions
How much does CLIB cost?
For 2026/27, tuition is €6,300 – €9,450 a year depending on age, paid in 10 monthly instalments of €630 – €945. In the Infant Department (€6,700) meals are included; for older students meals, uniform, bus and enrolment are charged separately.
Does CLIB offer the IB?
No. CLIB follows the Cambridge pathway — IGCSE, then AS and A Levels within the AICE Diploma. To compare the two systems, see our guide IB vs Cambridge.
Is CLIB recognised in Portugal?
Yes. CLIB is a registered international school recognised by the Portuguese Ministry of Education, so its certificates are valid in Portugal and students can apply to Portuguese universities or move into the national system.
How do admissions work?
Admissions are open all year. Families send an enquiry and visit the school; depending on age the child has a trial day or an interview, and may take an English assessment. A place is confirmed by signing the contract and paying the enrolment invoice.
