IPS Cascais British International School is one of the longest-established international schools in the Cascais area — founded in 1982 and now part of the Globeducate group, a network of 65+ international schools across 11 countries. The school follows the British National Curriculum from nursery through to Cambridge IGCSE and A-Levels, and serves around 440 students from over 40 nationalities.

The campus is purpose-built and eco-friendly, with basketball and tennis courts, a multipurpose sports field, outdoor learning areas, a library, and a 1,200m² multipurpose gymnasium and theatre currently under construction. Academic results are strong: 93% of students score above average in standardised English assessments, and 90% in Mathematics.

The after-school programme (VivaHUB) is one of the most comprehensive on offer in the area, covering sports, performing arts, coding, music, languages and craft activities. A new secondary campus is planned to open in September 2026, expanding provision for older students.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does IPS Cascais follow?
IPS Cascais British International School follows the British National Curriculum through Cambridge IGCSE and A-Levels. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does IPS Cascais cost per year?
Annual tuition at IPS Cascais British International School ranges from €11,049 to €17,811 per year depending on year group. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €4,000 and an annual re-enrollment fee of €950. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these change annually.
What ages does IPS Cascais accept?
IPS Cascais British International School accepts students from age 1 to 18. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where is IPS Cascais located?
IPS Cascais British International School is located at R. Lagoa 171, 2645-344 Alcabideche. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
