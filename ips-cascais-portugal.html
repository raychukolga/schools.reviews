IPS Cascais is an international school located in Cascais founded in 1982. The school follows the British/ A level curriculum, with English as the primary language of instruction.

The school accepts students from age 1 to 18. with approximately 440 students. Additional languages offered include Portuguese, Chinese, Dutch.
