Leiria International School (LIS) opened in 2023 and is the main international school in the Leiria region. Its first campus is in Marinha Grande, in a historic building from 1966 adapted for modern teaching; a second campus has since opened in Leiria.

LIS is an authorised Cambridge school from Early Years through Primary, Lower Secondary and IGCSE to AS and A Level, taught in English, with Singapore Maths built into the maths programme. The school is also a candidate for the IB Primary Years and Middle Years Programmes.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Leiria International School follow?
LIS follows the Cambridge pathway from Early Years to AS and A Level, taught in English, with Singapore Maths. It is also a candidate school for the IB PYP and MYP.
How much does Leiria International School cost per year?
The school does not publish its fees. Contact the admissions team for the current fee schedule.
Can graduates go to a Portuguese university?
Yes, with the Cambridge AICE Diploma or three A Levels, plus two years of residence in Portugal to apply under the same terms as national students.
Is there a school bus?
Yes. There is a Leiria route (Pousos – Leiria) and a Coast route (Caldas – Valado dos Frades – Nazaré – Pataias – Martingança – Moita), at extra cost.
