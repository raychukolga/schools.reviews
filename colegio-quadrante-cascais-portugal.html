Colégio Quadrante is a private Portuguese school in Pampilheira, Cascais, part of the To Be Schools group. It was formed from the merger of Colégio Europa and Escolinha Tia Ló, and takes children from the nursery to the end of Grade 6.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, with English every day and some subjects taught in English using the CLIL approach. Projects include education through art, digital education, food education, experimental science and environmental education.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Quadrante follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, with daily English and some subjects taught in English (CLIL).
How much does Colégio Quadrante cost?
In 2025/26 tuition was €5,005 (Grades 1–6) to €5,390 (baby room) a year, paid in 11 instalments, plus compulsory meals (€115–€180 a month) and enrollment.
Can children who do not speak Portuguese join?
The school offers Portuguese as a non-native language and describes itself as multicultural; ask the school about support for your child’s year.
