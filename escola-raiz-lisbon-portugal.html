Escola Raiz — today Raiz International Active Learning School — opened in Lisbon in 1998. Its founders studied school models in the USA, and one of them, psychologist Margarida Silveira Rodrigues, trained in Ypsilanti as a HighScope teacher and trainer. Raiz is the only Certified HighScope Center in Portugal.

The school follows the Portuguese national curriculum from pre-school to Grade 6, taught bilingually in Portuguese and English. Children learn through hands-on projects, discussion in small groups and outings, in a green setting in Restelo with a second building in Belém.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Escola Raiz cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — ask the school office. Meals (Fresh Menu) are included in the monthly fee, and income-based scholarships are available.
Is Escola Raiz an international school?
It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum, bilingually in Portuguese and English, with the American HighScope active-learning method. Children can move straight into any Portuguese school.
Up to what age does the school go?
From age 3 to Grade 6 (about 12 years old).
