Colégio da Via-Sacra is a Catholic private school in Viseu. It was founded in 1908 by Canon António Barreiros to give boys from the Beira region a family-style home while they studied at the city's lyceum, and in 1954 he left it to the Episcopal Seminary of Viseu.

From 1998 the school taught free of charge under a state contract (contrato de associação); for 2026/27 it publishes an annual fee. Today it teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from Grade 1 to Grade 9 (Grade 1–4 classes returned in 2010/11) and runs clubs, a music school and swimming.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio da Via-Sacra cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €3,750 a year for every grade (10 × €375), plus €200 on first enrollment and meals at €3 a day.
Which grades does the school teach?
Grades 1 to 9 (ages 6–15). There is no pre-school and no secondary (Grades 10–12).
Is there a discount for siblings?
Yes: with two children the elder gets 15% off; with three or four, the younger ones get 25% off.
