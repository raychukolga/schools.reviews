St Peter's International School is an international school in Palmela, Setúbal, founded in 1993. The school offers a bilingual Portuguese–English programme from as young as 4 months. At around age 6, families choose between two pathways: the International Pathway — leading to Cambridge IGCSE in middle school and the IB Diploma in Grades 11–12 — or the Portuguese National Curriculum. With 1,500 students from 44 nationalities on a 37,000 m² campus, it is one of the largest international schools in the Greater Lisbon area.

The school offers boarding from age 14 to 18, making it one of the very few international boarding schools in the Lisbon region. Additional languages offered include Spanish, Mandarin, German, and Latin.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does St Peter's International School follow?
St Peter's is bilingual Portuguese–English from 4 months to age 6. After that, families choose between the International Pathway (Cambridge IGCSE in middle school, followed by IB Diploma) or the Portuguese National Curriculum pathway. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does St Peter's International School cost per year?
Annual tuition at St Peter's International School ranges from €8,325 to €17,100 per year. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €800. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these can change annually.
What ages does St Peter's International School accept?
St Peter's International School accepts students from 4 months to 18 years. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where is St Peter's International School located?
St Peter's International School is located at Quinta dos Barreleiros CCI 3952, 2950-420 Palmela. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
Does St Peter's International School offer boarding?
Yes, St Peter's International School offers boarding for students aged 14 to 18. It is one of the very few international boarding schools in the Lisbon region. Please contact the school directly for boarding fees and availability.
