St Peter's International School is an international school located in Setubal founded in 1993. The school follows the Portuguese, IGCSE/Cambrige/IB Diploma curriculum, with English as the primary language of instruction.

The school accepts students from age 1 to 16. with approximately 1500 students. on a 37000 m² campus. Additional languages offered include Portuguese, Mandarin, German.
