The Instituto Español Giner de los Ríos is a Spanish state school abroad, owned and run by the Spanish Ministry of Education. It was founded in Lisbon in 1932 and named after Hermenegildo Giner de los Ríos in 1933. Since the mid-1970s it has been in Dafundo (Oeiras), on the Quinta de São João do Rio estate near the Tagus.

It is an integrated centre teaching the Spanish national curriculum from Educación Infantil (age 3) through Primaria, ESO and Bachillerato (age 18). Teaching is in Spanish, and Portuguese language is compulsory at every level.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does the Instituto Español Giner de los Ríos follow?
The full Spanish national curriculum — Infantil, Primaria, ESO and Bachillerato — taught in Spanish, with compulsory Portuguese.
How much does the Instituto Español cost per year?
For 2026/27, students without Spanish nationality pay €3,124 – €3,932 a year, depending on the stage. Spanish students pay separate complementary fees.
How do I apply?
Admission follows an annual procedure published by the Education Office of the Spanish Embassy in Portugal; places are limited.
Where is the Instituto Español located?
At Rua Direita do Dafundo 40, in Cruz Quebrada-Dafundo (Oeiras), on the western edge of Lisbon.
