Colégio Bissaya Barreto is a private Portuguese school in Bencanta, on the western edge of Coimbra. The Bissaya Barreto Foundation opened it in September 2003 to continue the work of the former Bencanta Institute for the Deaf and open it to all children.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from Grade 1 to Grade 9 and also runs Level IV vocational courses (thermal-spa technician and family & community support) for 15–20-year-olds. Lessons run 8:45–17:15, with early drop-off and late stay available.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Bissaya Barreto cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — contact the school office for the current price list. Clubs are included in the monthly fee; meals and sports/arts activities are extra.
What hours is the school open?
From 7:30 to 19:00, 1 September to 31 July. Lessons run 8:45–17:15.
Does the school have a bus?
Yes, school transport is available on registration; the route is set each year according to demand.
