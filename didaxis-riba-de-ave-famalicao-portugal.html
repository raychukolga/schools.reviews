Didáxis is a teaching cooperative founded on 15 July 1975 by a group of educators in the Ave valley. Its main campus in Riba de Ave, Vila Nova de Famalicão, grew from 1979 into a large school complex with a sports pavilion, canteen, library and secondary-school buildings.

The fee-paying regular school (“O Colégio”) teaches Grades 1–12 on the Portuguese national curriculum, with English integration, Cambridge certification and a Microsoft School programme. It runs its own bus network and, according to the school, leads Famalicão in secondary exam results.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Didáxis cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €3,025 (Grades 1–4), €3,120 (Grades 5–9) and €3,430 (Grades 10–12) a year, plus enrollment (€200) and meals.
Is Didáxis a private school?
Yes — the regular school is a fee-paying private cooperative school. The same cooperative also runs a separate, publicly funded vocational school.
Does Didáxis have school buses?
Yes, its own buses with seat belts, supervisors and Wi-Fi, €45–€110 a month depending on the zone.
