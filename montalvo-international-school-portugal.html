Montalvo International School is a small private secondary school in the historic centre of Loulé, in the Algarve. It was founded by its director, Walter Montalvo, who has taught in the Algarve since 2016, and opened with its first students in the 2024/25 school year.

The school follows the British Pearson Edexcel pathway: International GCSEs in Years 10 and 11 (ages 14–16), then A Levels in Years 12 and 13 (ages 16–18). Teaching is in English. The school also runs the Montalvo Partnership Program, which links students with local businesses for internships, job shadowing and community projects.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Montalvo International School follow?
Montalvo follows the British Pearson Edexcel pathway: International GCSEs in Years 10–11 and A Levels in Years 12–13, taught in English.
How much does Montalvo International School cost per year?
According to a third-party directory, annual tuition for 2025/26 was €11,250 – €13,785. The school does not publish fees, so confirm current figures directly.
What ages does Montalvo International School take?
Montalvo is a secondary-only school for students aged roughly 14 to 18 (Years 10–13).
Where is Montalvo International School located?
Montalvo International School is at Av. Marçal Pacheco 37, 8100-567 Loulé, in the centre of the town.
