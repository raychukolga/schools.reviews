Colégio Moderno is a private Portuguese school in Campo Grande, Lisbon, founded in 1936 by João Soares. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from the nursery to the end of secondary school.

The school describes itself as secular (escola laica) and open to all faiths. Its project is built on humanist values and gives a central role to education through the arts; it also runs its own music school. Around 140 teachers work at the school.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Moderno follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from the nursery to Grade 12.
How much does Colégio Moderno cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,400 (pre-school) to €6,500 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 monthly instalments, plus €470 a year for enrollment, insurance and the annual fee. The nursery costs €530 a month over 12 months.
Is Colégio Moderno a religious school?
No. The school describes itself as secular and open to all faiths and religions.
Does Colégio Moderno have a school bus?
Yes. The school bus costs €135 a month for one trip a day or €166 for two.
