St. George's School is a bilingual Cambridge International School in Cobre, Cascais, for Years 6 to 13. It shares its street with St James' Primary School, and the two schools work in partnership so that children can move on from primary without changing setting.

The curriculum, built with St James', centres on English, Portuguese, Mathematics and Sciences, supported by the English National Curriculum. Pupils sit Cambridge Checkpoint in Years 6 and 9, IGCSEs in Year 11 and AS and A Levels in Years 12–13.

Frequently asked questions
How much does St. George's School cost?
In 2025/26 €790 a month for Years 6–9, €815 for Years 10–11 and €925 for Years 12–13, paid over 10 months — or €7,742 to €9,065 for the year paid in advance. New students pay an €800 enrolment fee.
How is St. George's connected to St James' Primary School?
The two schools are next to each other in Cobre, Cascais, and work in partnership: St James' teaches the primary years and St. George's continues from Year 6 to Year 13 with a curriculum developed together.
What exams do pupils take?
Cambridge Checkpoint in Years 6 and 9, IGCSEs in Year 11 and AS and A Levels in Years 12–13.
