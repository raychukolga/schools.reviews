Colégio de Santa Maria is a private Catholic school in Lapa, Lisbon, with two buildings on Rua das Praças and Rua de S. Félix. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 9.

It belongs to Associação 31 de Maio, a non-profit association recognised as a public-utility organisation. Its educational approach follows Father Joseph Kentenich, founder of the Schoenstatt Movement.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio de Santa Maria follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 9.
How much does Colégio de Santa Maria cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,320 (pre-school) to €6,360 (Grades 5–9) a year, paid in 10 monthly instalments, plus €395 a year for enrollment and insurance.
Does Colégio de Santa Maria go up to Grade 12?
No. The school ends at Grade 9 (age 15).
