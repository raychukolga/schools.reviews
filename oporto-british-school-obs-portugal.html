Oporto British School (OBS) is an international school located in Porto founded in 1894. The school follows the CIS, COBIS, IB, HMC curriculum, with English as the primary language of instruction.

The school's facilities include The school has a privileged location in the community of Foz do Douro. With the Atlantic Ocean within sight of the front gate, the children enjoy learning in a beautiful setting..

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. with approximately 577 students. Additional languages offered include Portuguese.
