Externato Dom Fuas Roupinho (EDFR) is a private school in Nazaré, founded in 1958. For decades it was the main secondary school of the area; today it focuses entirely on vocational secondary courses and education-and-training courses (CEF) for young people, plus certified modular training for adults.

Courses run for three years (Grades 10–12) and combine general subjects with technical training and work placements. The courses are publicly funded, co-financed by the European Social Fund (FSE+), so students do not pay tuition.

Frequently asked questions
Is Externato Dom Fuas Roupinho free?
Yes — its vocational courses are publicly funded and co-financed by the EU, so students pay no tuition.
What courses does Externato Dom Fuas Roupinho offer?
Three-year vocational courses (Grades 10–12) such as beautician, pharmacy assistant, fashion design, software development, management, and sales and marketing.
Does Externato Dom Fuas Roupinho teach younger children?
No — today it offers only vocational secondary and training courses for young people from about 15, and courses for adults.
