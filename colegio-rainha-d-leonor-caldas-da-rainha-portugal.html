Colégio Rainha D. Leonor (CRDL) is a private Portuguese school in Caldas da Rainha, in the Oeste region north of Lisbon. It opened in the 2005/06 school year with about 400 pupils and now takes children from the nursery (from 8 months) to Grade 12, with academic and vocational courses in secondary.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum and is a Cambridge International School, using a blended Cambridge curriculum for literacy and maths combined with project-based learning. Pupils from Grade 3 to Grade 9 work with iPads in class (iCRDL project), and the school runs integrated music and dance courses (ensino articulado).

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Rainha D. Leonor follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, combined with a blended Cambridge curriculum — the school is a Cambridge International School.
How much does Colégio Rainha D. Leonor cost?
In 2026/27 tuition for new pupils is €4,950–€5,400 a year depending on the level, plus enrollment (€370), meals and, from Grade 3, the iPad package.
Where is Colégio Rainha D. Leonor?
In Caldas da Rainha, in the Oeste region of the district of Leiria, about an hour north of Lisbon by the A8.
