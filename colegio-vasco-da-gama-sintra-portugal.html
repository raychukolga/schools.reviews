Colégio Vasco da Gama is a private Portuguese school in Meleças, in the municipality of Sintra, founded in 1959 by Dr. João António Nabais. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, and is part of International Schools Partnership (iSP).

The school stands on an estate of about 4.5 hectares with a riding school, a tennis school and a half-Olympic swimming pool. English is taught from pre-school with Cambridge materials, and pupils can take Cambridge English exams from YLE to CAE.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Vasco da Gama follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, with Cambridge English.
Where is Colégio Vasco da Gama?
In Meleças, Belas, in the municipality of Sintra — close to the Mira-Sintra/Meleças station on the Sintra train line and the A16.
How much does Colégio Vasco da Gama cost?
The school does not publish its tuition fees online — ask the school. Meals are included in pre-school and cost €150–€186 a month from Grade 1.
