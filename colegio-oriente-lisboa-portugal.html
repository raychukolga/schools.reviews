Colégio Oriente is a private Portuguese school in Parque das Nações, Lisbon, founded in 2009 as a partnership between two Portuguese companies. It received its definitive licence in 2010. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from the nursery to Grade 12.

Children can start in the baby room from 6 months. The school has its own psychologist for support and careers guidance, and runs after-school academies in sport, music, languages and technology.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Oriente follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from the nursery to Grade 12.
How much does Colégio Oriente cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €5,700 (pre-school) to €6,500 (secondary) a year; the nursery costs €6,960 and the baby room €8,160. New pupils pay €450 to enrol, then €350 a year to renew.
From what age does Colégio Oriente take children?
From 6 months, in the baby room.
