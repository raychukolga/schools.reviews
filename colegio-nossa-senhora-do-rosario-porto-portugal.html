Colégio de Nossa Senhora do Rosário is a Catholic private school on Avenida da Boavista in Porto, owned by the Institute of the Religious of the Sacred Heart of Mary (RSCM) — the same congregation that runs Colégio do Sagrado Coração de Maria in Lisbon. Its roots go back to 1872; it took its current name in 1926 and moved to its present building, on a 20,000 m² site, in 1958.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum from pre-school to Grade 12. English is a signature: daily from age 3, taught in level groups, with CLIL in primary and Cambridge certification from Starters to Proficiency. A swimming-pool building opened in 2010.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio do Rosário cost?
In 2026/27 the monthly fee is €604.50 (pre-school) to €687.50 (secondary) — about €6,000–€6,900 a year over 10 months — plus enrollment (€440 the first year).
What curriculum does Colégio do Rosário follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, with a strong English programme and Cambridge English exams.
Is Colégio do Rosário a Catholic school?
Yes — it belongs to the Religious of the Sacred Heart of Mary, and faith formation is part of school life.
