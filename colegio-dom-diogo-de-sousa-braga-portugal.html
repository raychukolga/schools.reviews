Colégio Dom Diogo de Sousa is a Catholic private school in São Vicente, Braga, owned by the Archdiocese of Braga. It opened in September 1949 as a boys’ school founded by a group of Catholic educators led by Dr. Mário Afonso and Father Joaquim António Alves, and is named after the reforming 16th-century Archbishop of Braga.

Today it is co-educational and follows the Portuguese national curriculum from pre-school to Grade 12, with a humanist, explicitly Catholic educational project. Extracurricular activities, clubs, catechesis and holiday programmes are organised through its Academia 360.
