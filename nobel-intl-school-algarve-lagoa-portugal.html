Nobel Int'l School Algarve — Lagoa is an international school located in Lagoa founded in 1972. The school follows the British, Portuguese curriculum, with English Portuguese as the primary language of instruction.

The school accepts students from age 3 to 18. Additional languages offered include Portuguese, German, French.
