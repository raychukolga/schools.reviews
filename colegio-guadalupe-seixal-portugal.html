Colégio Guadalupe is a private Portuguese school in Corroios, Seixal, set in the Aroeira pine forest on the south bank of the Tagus. It was founded in 2000 by the children of teacher Maria de Guadalupe, who had founded the earlier Externato Infante D. Henrique and Externato Novo Dia.

From Grade 1 families choose between the Portuguese national curriculum, taught fully in Portuguese, and a dual-certification curriculum (since 2019) that combines it with the Cambridge Pathway, with part of the lessons in English and the Cambridge AICE Diploma at the end of secondary. All pre-school children start Cambridge Stage 1 in their final year.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Guadalupe follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, or a dual-certification track that combines it with the Cambridge curriculum and leads to the Cambridge AICE Diploma.
How much does Colégio Guadalupe cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — ask the admissions team (informacoes@c-guadalupe.com). Tuition is paid in 10 monthly instalments.
Can children who do not speak Portuguese join?
Yes — about 20% of pupils are international, and Portuguese as a non-native language is taught in small groups by level after a placement test.
