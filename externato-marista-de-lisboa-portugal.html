Externato Marista de Lisboa is a private Catholic school run by the Marist Brothers in São Domingos de Benfica, Lisbon. It opened in 1947 and is co-educational, from pre-school to Grade 12. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese.

It belongs to the Marist Province of Compostela, which also runs the Colégio Marista de Carcavelos. Pastoral, solidarity and Eco-Schools projects are part of school life.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Externato Marista de Lisboa follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12.
How much does Externato Marista de Lisboa cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €4,750 (pre-school) to €5,790 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 monthly instalments, plus an annual enrollment fee of €435–€475.
Is Externato Marista de Lisboa a religious school?
Yes. It is a Catholic school run by the Marist Brothers.
