Colégio Ribadouro is a private Portuguese school on Rua de Santa Catarina in central Porto, licensed since 1970. It began with evening classes for working students and was relaunched from the 1980s by a group of teachers focused on competences and learning; today it draws pupils from across the Porto area and has expanded its buildings.

It teaches from pre-school to Grade 12 on the Portuguese national curriculum, with extra teaching hours in core subjects, English as a Cambridge educational partner, CLIL, and in secondary split classes, a wide choice of optional subjects and individual university-entry guidance.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Ribadouro cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €4,455 (pre-school and Grades 1–4) to €5,940 (secondary) a year, paid in 11 instalments, plus enrollment (€360) and meals.
What curriculum does Colégio Ribadouro follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, with extra hours in core subjects and Cambridge English.
Where is Colégio Ribadouro?
On Rua de Santa Catarina in central Porto, near the Marquês metro station.
