Colégio Atlântico is a private Portuguese school in Pinhal de Frades, Seixal, on the south bank of the Tagus. It takes children from the nursery (creche) to the end of Grade 12.

Up to Grade 9 it follows the Portuguese national curriculum with a certified bilingual English programme (Cambridge University learning partner). In secondary, students choose between the national curriculum (science, socio-economics or languages and humanities) and the IB Diploma Programme — the school is an IB World School. It also takes part in Erasmus+ exchanges and is a UNESCO associated school.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Atlântico follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, with bilingual English; in secondary students can choose the IB Diploma Programme instead.
How much does Colégio Atlântico cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €4,664–€4,884 a year up to Grade 12 on the national curriculum and €8,580 for the IB Diploma, paid in 11 instalments, plus enrollment (€400) and meals (€145 a month).
Where is Colégio Atlântico?
In Pinhal de Frades, Seixal, on the south bank of the Tagus, in the district of Setúbal.
