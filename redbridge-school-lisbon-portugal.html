Redbridge School Lisbon is one of a very small number of schools in Portugal offering genuinely trilingual education across three languages — French, Portuguese and English — all used as active languages of instruction, not merely taught as foreign languages. Founded in 2017, the school is located in central Lisbon and serves around 510 students from age 3 to 17.

What sets Redbridge apart is both the linguistic depth and the choice of curriculum pathway. Families can enrol on the French/English track (following the French national curriculum), the Portuguese/English track (following the Portuguese national system), or the Cambridge track — leading to IGCSE and IB Diploma in the senior years. The French track is accredited by the AEFE (Agence pour l'enseignement français à l'étranger), meaning it meets the standards of the French Ministry of Education — a significant quality marker.

The school is particularly well suited to families from French-speaking backgrounds, families who want their children to maintain strong Portuguese alongside English, or those seeking maximum flexibility across three educational systems within a single school.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Redbridge School Lisbon follow?
Redbridge School Lisbon follows the Cambridge / French/ Portuguese curriculum. Always verify the latest programme details directly with the school, as curricula can evolve.
How much does Redbridge School Lisbon cost per year?
Annual tuition at Redbridge School Lisbon ranges from €8,900 to €18,000 per year. There is a one-time enrollment fee of €4,000 and an application fee of €200. Always verify current fees directly with the school, as these can change annually.
What ages does Redbridge School Lisbon accept?
Redbridge School Lisbon accepts students from age 3 to 17. Please contact the school directly to confirm current availability for specific age groups.
Where is Redbridge School Lisbon located?
Redbridge School Lisbon is located at R. Francisco Metrass 97, 1350-164 Lisboa. Please check Google Maps or the school website for precise directions and parking information.
Is school bus transport available at Redbridge School Lisbon?
Redbridge School Lisbon offers transport: they have partnered with a Bus Service. Fees may apply. Verify current routes and pricing with the school.
