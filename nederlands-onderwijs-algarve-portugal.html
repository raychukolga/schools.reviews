Nederlands Onderwijs Algarve Portugal (NOAP) is a Dutch day school in the countryside near Silves, in the western Algarve. It is run by the Dutch foundation Stichting NOAP, with a volunteer board, and is affiliated with NOB (Nederlands Onderwijs in het Buitenland). Since September 2024 the school has been in a larger building on its own grounds.

Primary pupils (groups 1–8) follow the full Dutch curriculum in Dutch, taught only by qualified teachers. All pupils also have Portuguese lessons twice a week and learn about Portuguese culture. Secondary pupils study with the Dutch Wereldschool programme towards the Staatsexamen, supported by mentors and extra subjects such as Portuguese, sport, cooking and project work.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Nederlands Onderwijs Algarve follow?
NOAP teaches the Dutch primary curriculum in Dutch (groups 1–8). Secondary pupils follow the Dutch Wereldschool programme towards the Staatsexamen, with mentors at school.
Can my child go back to a Dutch school afterwards?
Yes. NOAP is affiliated with NOB and inspected by the Dutch Education Inspectorate, so pupils can return to the Dutch school system without a gap.
Can we enrol for only part of the year?
Yes. The minimum stay is one term; families can also enrol for a semester or the full year.
Where is Nederlands Onderwijs Algarve located?
The school is in the countryside near Silves, in the western Algarve. Ask the school for the exact address and directions.
