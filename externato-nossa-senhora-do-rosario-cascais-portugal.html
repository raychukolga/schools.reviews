Externato Nossa Senhora do Rosário is a private Catholic school in Bairro do Rosário, Cascais, run by the Salesian Sisters (Daughters of Mary Help of Christians). It has been in Bairro do Rosário since 1976 and follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese.

Since 2022/23 it teaches Grades 5 to 12, after opening secondary school in 2022. Pupils can join a Dual Diploma programme for a second, US high-school diploma.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Externato Nossa Senhora do Rosário follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from Grade 5 to Grade 12.
How much does it cost?
For 2026/27 tuition is €405 a month in Grades 5–6, €425 in Grades 7–9 and €495 in secondary, plus enrollment (€380–€460) and €30 insurance.
Does the school have pre-school or primary?
No. Since 2022/23 it teaches Grades 5 to 12 only.
