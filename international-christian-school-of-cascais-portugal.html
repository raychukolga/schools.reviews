International Christian School of Cascais is an international school located in Cascais founded in 1990. The school follows the American curriculum.

Christian values, No enrollment period - students are accepted all year-round. The Planalto, after pre-school, is intended for boys.

Additional languages offered include English, Portuguese, Chinese, Afrikaans.
