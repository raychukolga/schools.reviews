International Christian School of Cascais (ICSC) is a Christian international school founded in 1980 and located in Alcabideche, Cascais. ICSC provides an English-medium, North American-style education from Pre-K through Grade 12, combining an American high school diploma with Christian values in a supportive, multicultural learning environment.

The school serves the international community, including missionary families, and welcomes students from a wide range of nationalities and educational backgrounds. ICSC is a small school with capacity for approximately 80 students, and class sizes are deliberately kept small: a maximum of 8 students in Kindergarten, generally no more than 10 in Grades 1 & 2, and a maximum of 14 in Grades 3–12.
