Bright International School Algarve is a Cambridge international school in Loulé. It opened in 2019 as Eupheus International School and was taken over by the Portuguese Sharing Education Group in 2024, when it was renamed Bright. The principal is Tom Caston, previously with the British schools group MPW.

The school follows the Cambridge pathway from Early Years (age 3) through Primary and Lower Secondary to Cambridge IGCSE at 14–16. Cambridge Advanced (A Levels, ages 16–19) opens in September 2027. Teaching is in English, with ESL support for students who are still learning the language.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Bright International School follow?
Bright follows the Cambridge International pathway — Early Years, Primary, Lower Secondary and Cambridge IGCSE. Cambridge A Levels start in September 2027.
How much does Bright International School cost per year?
For 2026/27, yearly tuition is €7,750 – €15,360 depending on the year group, plus a €1,000 registration fee every year. Lunch and most after-school activities are extra.
Is Bright the same school as Eupheus International School?
Yes. Eupheus International School opened in Loulé in 2019; Sharing Education Group took it over in 2024 and renamed it Bright International School.
Where is Bright International School located?
The school is at Parque Industrial de Loulé, Zona Industrial B, Lote 14, 8100-272 Loulé.
