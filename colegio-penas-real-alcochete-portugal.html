✓ What we like

Countryside campus in Alcochete with lots of outdoor space, about 25 minutes from Lisbon over the Vasco da Gama bridge
Nursery to Grade 6 on one site — siblings of different ages can attend together

◎ Worth noting
The school stops at Grade 6 — families need another school from Grade 7
Fees are paid over 12 months, so the annual cost is higher than at schools that charge 10 instalments
Lessons are in Portuguese — children need to speak Portuguese or be ready to learn it

Colégio Penas Real opened in 2008/09 in Alcochete, on the south bank of the Tagus, with a nursery, pre-school and primary school. In 2013/14 it added Grades 5–6.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum in Portuguese. The school's motto is "Acreditamos no futuro!" — it combines academic standards with projects, theatre and visual arts, and children spend much of the day outdoors.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Penas Real cost?
In 2026/27 €590 a month for nursery and pre-school and €600 a month for Grades 1–6, paid over 12 months (€7,080 and €7,200 a year). Meals and the day from 8:00 to 18:30 are included.
What ages does the school take?
From nursery up to Grade 6 (about 12 years old).
Is there a school bus?
Yes, the school runs school transport — ask the school about routes and prices.
