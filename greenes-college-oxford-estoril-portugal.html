Greene's College Oxford is a British tutorial college founded in Oxford in 1967 by Edward Greene. It brought its Oxford tutorial method to Estoril in 2015, and has since opened a second Portuguese centre in Príncipe Real, Lisbon (October 2023), as well as a centre in the Algarve.

Instead of classes, students learn in tutorials — one-to-one or in small groups of two or three — with a Personal Tutor who meets them every week. Full-time students take International GCSEs (ages 14–16) or three A Levels (16–18); the college also offers A Level retakes, a Foundation Year, vocational diplomas, online sixth form and part-time tuition.

Frequently asked questions
What does Greene's College Oxford — Estoril offer?
Full-time International GCSEs (ages 14–16) and A Levels (16–18) taught in tutorials of one to three students, plus A Level retakes, a Foundation Year and part-time tuition.
How much does Greene's Estoril cost per year?
Full-time IGCSE costs €18,575 and A Levels €24,360 a year, plus a €2,400 registration fee, subject supplements, science practicals and exam fees.
Can students join in the middle of the year?
Yes. Students can join after September on 10-, 20- or 30-week programmes, with fees calculated per subject.
Where is Greene's Estoril located?
At Rua Dom Afonso Henriques 1614, 2765-576 Estoril. Greene’s also has a centre in Príncipe Real, Lisbon.
