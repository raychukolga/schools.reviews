Grande Colégio Universal is a private Portuguese school on Rua da Boavista in central Porto. It was founded in 1910 by Father Santos Brito, Father Fonseca e Pinho and the lawyer Morais de Almeida, began as a boys’ boarding school and has been led by the Brito family for about 50 years.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, with English throughout and Cambridge English exams. The campus has two modern buildings, a library and IT room, and outdoor courts for basketball, handball, football and volleyball.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Grande Colégio Universal cost?
In 2026/27 tuition is €4,170 (pre-school and Grades 1–4) to €4,920 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 instalments, plus an annual enrollment of €341–€390.
What curriculum does Grande Colégio Universal follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12.
Is there a uniform?
No full uniform — a school smock in pre-school and primary, and the school tracksuit for PE.
