Colégio de Nossa Senhora da Bonança is a Catholic private school in Mafamude, Vila Nova de Gaia, run by the Franciscan Hospitaller Sisters of the Immaculate Conception (CONFHIC). Its Ministry of Education licence dates from 1949, and its central building is a historic palacete with a chapel, classrooms and a boarding wing.

It teaches from the nursery (from 4 months) to Grade 12 on the Portuguese national curriculum, and also runs a girls’ boarding house. Families can apply for state support (contrato simples / de desenvolvimento) that reduces fees depending on income.
