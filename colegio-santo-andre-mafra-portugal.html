Colégio Santo André is a private school in Venda do Pinheiro, in the municipality of Mafra, about 25 minutes north of Lisbon. Its motto is "Saber e Ser".

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum from Grade 1 to Grade 12. In primary the school adds its own subjects, including emotional education, and it takes part in Erasmus+.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio Santo André cost?
For new pupils in 2026/27 €460 a month in Grades 1–4 and 7–9 and €455 in Grades 5–6, paid over 11 months (about €5,000 a year). Pupils who stay in the same cycle pay a lower fixed fee.
Is secondary school free?
The price list charges no tuition for Grades 10–12 — ask the school about how secondary places are allocated.
Where is the school?
In Venda do Pinheiro (Mafra), about 25 minutes north of Lisbon.
