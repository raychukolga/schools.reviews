Colégio Marista de Carcavelos is a private Catholic school of the Marist Brothers in Parede, between Carcavelos and Cascais. It opened in 1965, has been co-educational since 1971 and today has about 1,700 pupils from age 3 to Grade 12. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese.

Its grounds include an educational farm with animals and vegetable gardens, used for weekly outdoor lessons in primary. The school belongs to the Marist Province of Compostela, which also runs the Externato Marista de Lisboa.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Marista de Carcavelos follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from age 3 to Grade 12.
How much does Colégio Marista de Carcavelos cost?
The school does not publish its fees online — ask the school office.
When are new places available?
New classes open mainly at age 3 (two classes), age 5 (one class) and Grade 5 (two classes); other places depend on vacancies.
