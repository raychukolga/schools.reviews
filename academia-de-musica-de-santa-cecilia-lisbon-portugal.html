Academia de Música de Santa Cecília was founded in 1964 by Vera Franco Nogueira as a non-profit private school combining the regular curriculum with specialist music teaching. It is run by the Associação da Academia de Música de Santa Cecília and is declared of public interest.

It teaches children from age 3 to Grade 12. Pupils can follow the general curriculum or the integrated music course, where academic and music studies share one timetable. The school is known for its exam results and regularly tops Portugal's secondary school rankings.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Academia de Música de Santa Cecília cost?
In 2026/27 from €574 a month in pre-school to €673 a month in secondary, paid over 10 months. On the secondary music course with Ministry support the monthly fee is €254.
Do children need to play an instrument to apply?
The music courses have entrance tests; the school publishes the test outlines on its website.
Is music teaching free?
Specialist music teaching on the integrated course is funded by the Ministry of Education, subject to available places. Parents pay for the general curriculum.
