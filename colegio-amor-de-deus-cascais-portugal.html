Colégio Amor de Deus is a private Catholic school in Alcabideche, Cascais, part of the Amor de Deus schools. It follows the Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12, and is co-educational.

With about 1,300 pupils across five levels, it is one of the larger private schools in the Cascais area. It has its own swimming pool and runs sports, dance, music and robotics activities after school.

Frequently asked questions
What curriculum does Colégio Amor de Deus follow?
The Portuguese national curriculum, taught in Portuguese, from pre-school to Grade 12.
How much does Colégio Amor de Deus cost per year?
For 2026/27 tuition is €4,230 (Grades 1–4) to €5,270 (secondary) a year, paid in 10 monthly instalments, plus enrollment (€360 new, €335 renewal) and €30 insurance.
Is Colégio Amor de Deus a religious school?
Yes. It is a Catholic school of the Amor de Deus congregation.
