Colégio João Paulo II is a small private school in Pedra Mourinha, Portimão, opened on 14 August 2008 as a family project with a nursery, pre-school and primary school (1º ciclo). It holds a definitive Ministry of Education authorisation for pre-school and primary.

It follows the Portuguese national curriculum with a broad set of included activities: double teaching in primary, English, music, physical education, meditation, philosophy for children and IT, and French in Grades 3–4.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Colégio João Paulo II cost?
The latest published table (2024/25) shows €355–€375 a month over 12 months — about €4,260–€4,500 a year, plus enrollment and meals.
What ages does Colégio João Paulo II take?
Nursery (from about 1 year), pre-school and primary Grades 1–4.
Where is Colégio João Paulo II?
In Pedra Mourinha, Portimão, in the western Algarve.
