Colégio de Nossa Senhora do Alto is a Catholic private school of the Diocese of the Algarve, on the Alto hill in the east of Faro. Part of the school is housed in the Palácio do Alto.

It teaches the Portuguese national curriculum in Portuguese. We are still requesting details from the school — fees, grade range and school hours will be added once confirmed.
