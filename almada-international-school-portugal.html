Almada International School (AIS) is a bilingual international school in Sobreda, Almada, on the south bank of the Tagus. It takes children from age 1 in the nursery, through kindergarten and Cambridge Primary, and from 2026/27 is opening Cambridge Lower Secondary for ages 11–13.

Children learn in English and Portuguese, and the school adds French and digital and AI literacy as its "languages of the future". Teaching is hands-on and project-based, with a strong focus on nature: the campus has a farm and outdoor spaces used every day.

Frequently asked questions
How much does Almada International School cost?
In 2026/27 from €698 a month for the youngest children to €1,098 a month in Lower Secondary — €7,678 to €10,980 a year, with meals included.
What languages do children learn in?
English and Portuguese, in a bilingual programme from nursery, with French added as a third language.
Up to what age does the school go?
From age 1 to Year 7 (about 13) from 2026/27, when Lower Secondary opens. The school says this stage prepares pupils for IGCSE.
